Neighborly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Neighborly is a neighborhood help app where neighbors can post and browse small service requests such as moving, repairs, tutoring, gardening and cleaning. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

Neighborly does not require you to create an account, and we do not run a server that stores your personal data. Requests you create and messages you type are saved only in your own device’s browser storage so the app can remember them. We cannot see this data, and you can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s data in your device settings.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Unity Ads. Unity may collect and use certain device information, such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address and general usage data, to deliver and measure ads and to show ads that may be more relevant to you. You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device’s Android settings. For details, see Unity’s privacy policy at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Location

Neighborly does not access your device’s location. Distances and district names shown in the app are sample content.

Children

Neighborly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the advertising provider described above, as needed to show ads.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date of the last update shown above.
